Divyang UDID Card Renewal & Lost (दिव्यांग कार्ड रिन्यूअल व सुधार) - Complete Guide

Divyang sadasyo ke Unique Disability ID card ki expiry par renewal, khoye hue card ki duplicate copy aur address correction online apply karein.

How to Apply (Step by Step)

1
Swavlamban card portal par login karein.
2
UDID Number aur Date of Birth enter karein.
3
'Apply for Card Renewal' ya 'Surrender/Correction' chunein.
4
CMO / Medical Board approval ke baad naya digital card download karein.
