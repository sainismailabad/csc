4. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectify inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@ibadtechcsc.com.

5. CPRA Compliance (California Users)

California residents have additional rights under the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including:
