Image Popup Manager
Image Popup / Banner Yeh feature website load hone par ek full-image popup modal show karta hai — jaise koi result link, advertisement, ya special notice. User ek baar close karega to 24 ghante tak nahi dikhega (per-user memory).
Important Tips
  • Image URL: Full URL with https:// (example: https://i.imgur.com/abc.jpg)
  • Test: Browser mein URL paste karke pehle check karein ki image khul rahi hai ya nahi
  • Recommended size: 800x600 or 1200x900 pixels
  • Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP — sab chalega
  • Ek baar mein sirf 1 popup active rakhein — multiple active mein top priority wala show hoga

AdSense Approval Checklist
1

Required Pages

About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, Terms & Conditions — ye 5 pages zaroor honi chahiye.

2

20+ Quality Posts

Har post minimum 800+ words. Original content — copy-paste bilkul na karein.

3

Traffic Generate Karein

Google Search Console, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram par content share karein.

4

AdSense Apply Karein

adsense.google.com → Sign up → Site URL add → Wait 1-2 weeks.

5

Publisher ID Copy

AdSense Dashboard → Account → Account Information → ca-pub-XXXXXXXX

6

Ad Units Create

AdSense Dashboard → Ads → By ad unit → Create new → Display ads → Copy data-ad-slot.
