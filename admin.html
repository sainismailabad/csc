AdSense Approval Guide
1

Required Pages Banayein

About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, Terms & Conditions — ye 5 pages zaroor honi chahiye.

2

20+ Quality Posts Publish Karein

Har post minimum 800+ words ka ho. Original content likhein — copy-paste bilkul na karein.

3

Traffic Generate Karein

Google Search Console mein site submit karein. WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram par content share karein.

4

AdSense Apply Karein

adsense.google.com par jayein → Sign up → Site URL add karein → Wait for approval (1-2 weeks).

5

Publisher ID Copy Karein

Approval ke baad AdSense Dashboard → Account → Account Information mein ca-pub-XXXXXXXX milega. Ye upar wale field mein paste karein.

6

Ad Units Create Karein

AdSense Dashboard → Ads → By ad unit → Create new ad unit → Display ads → Copy the data-ad-slot number.
