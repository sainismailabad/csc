Who We Are

IBAD TECH CSC is a trusted Common Service Center (CSC) and information portal dedicated to helping Indian citizens access government services, job notifications, welfare schemes, and financial guidance in simple, easy-to-understand language.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between government services and common citizens by providing accurate, timely, and reliable information.

Our Mission

  • Provide accurate and up-to-date government job notifications
  • Simplify complex government schemes into easy language
  • Guide citizens on financial planning and government benefits
  • Offer 24/7 support for government service applications
  • Build trust through verified and reliable information
